Study strategy

Three phases designed around the supplied portions and the December exam window.

01

Build the base · 15 Oct–2 Nov

Work through the foundations in Mathematics, Economics, Physics and English. Start short, regular recall for History and Malayalam.

02

Finish every module · 3–20 Nov

Complete the remaining portions, internal-only Mathematics topics, Economics issues, and Physics spreadsheet activities. Tick a module only when you can retrieve it without notes.

03

Revise and practise · 21–30 Nov

Rotate all six subjects, write timed answers, repeat graphs and calculations, then use the mixed mock and error log on 30 November.

Weekly rhythm

Use the heavier study blocks for Mathematics, Economics and Physics calculations. Rotate History, English and Malayalam across the second block and the lighter recall block. Keep one short Sunday review, then take the rest of the day off.

For each topic

  1. Read the prescribed portion and mark unfamiliar terms.
  2. Close the notes and explain or write the idea from memory.
  3. Solve a problem, sketch a curve, or draft a short answer.
  4. Record one mistake and retry it during the next review.
